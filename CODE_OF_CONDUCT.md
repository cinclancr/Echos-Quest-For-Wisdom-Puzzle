# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are a small research community around an open puzzle. Everyone is
welcome regardless of background, and everyone should be able to
participate without harassment.

## Standards

* Be kind and assume good faith.
* Critique methods, not people.
* Do not post other people's key material, doxx puzzle authors, or harass
  the puzzle's creator.
* Unsolicited advertising, off-topic crypto price talk and personal attacks
  are unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Report issues via GitHub issue trackers or the repository contacts; the
maintainers will respond. Serious violations may lead to blocks from the
repository.
